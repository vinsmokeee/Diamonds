FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 1–5 minutes after payment. Use the order link to track status.

I entered the wrong ID. What now?

Always press “Check” and confirm the nickname before paying. Diamonds sent to a valid but wrong account can’t be reversed.

Payment went through but no diamonds?

Open your order page. If it shows “failed”, contact support with your order ID and we’ll retry or refund.

Do you need my password?

Never. We only need your User ID and Zone ID.
