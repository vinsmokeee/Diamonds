FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Once we confirm your bank payment, diamonds are usually sent within 5–30 minutes during working hours. Use your order link to track it.

Which banks can I pay with?

Scan our QR code with your mobile banking app and pay the exact amount shown. Keep your receipt and enter the journal number on the payment page.

I entered the wrong ID. What now?

Message us in the chat right away, before we deliver. Diamonds sent to a valid but wrong account can’t be reversed.

Payment went through but no diamonds?

Open the chat (bottom-right) and send us a photo of your receipt. We’ll check it and reply right there.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, if we couldn’t deliver, or you ask before we deliver. Diamonds already delivered to the ID you entered can’t be refunded. See our Terms & Refund Policy.

Do you need my password?

Never. We only need your User ID and Zone ID.
