1. Who we are

SOMA Diamonds is an independent top-up service in Bhutan. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Moonton. Mobile Legends: Bang Bang and its diamonds are the property of their owner. We buy top-ups from an authorised supplier and deliver them to the game account you give us.

2. Placing an order

  • You give us your game name, User ID and Zone ID, and choose a package. Prices are in Ngultrum (Nu.) and are shown before you pay.
  • You are responsible for entering the correct ID. We deliver exactly to the ID on the order.
  • Some packages may be temporarily unavailable when our supplier's prices change. A package you have already paid for is not affected.
  • Prices can change at any time, but you always pay the price shown when you placed the order.

3. Payment

  • Pay by scanning our bank QR with your mobile banking app and entering the exact amount shown.
  • Send us your payment receipt (screenshot) through the site. We check every payment against our bank account before delivering.
  • Each receipt and journal number can be used for one order only. Reused, edited or fake receipts are rejected, and the order is cancelled.
  • Paid less than the price? We'll tell you in the chat how much is still due. Your diamonds are sent once the full amount arrives.
  • Paid more than the price? We send the extra back to the account you paid from.

4. Delivery

  • After we confirm your payment, diamonds are usually delivered within minutes. During busy times or outside working hours it can take longer.
  • You can follow your order on the order page and in the chat. If you don't see the diamonds, restart the game first.
  • If you have already bought a first-purchase bonus or a pass in the game, the game's own rules decide whether a bonus applies again. We can't change that.
